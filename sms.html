BabyBat Game Hub

SMS Notifications

BabyBat is a private, invitation-only game platform for adult participants. SMS is used only for opted-in transactional game and account notifications.

Transactional alerts only

How BabyBat uses text messaging

Participants may choose to receive SMS alerts so they do not have to keep the BabyBat app open. Messages are limited to game and account events such as:

  • new BabyBat Mail or a reply
  • a newly issued Directive or submission update
  • a scoring notice or reward unlock
  • an organization OPEN/CLOSED status change
  • a deadline or other requested game reminder

No marketing or promotional advertising messages are sent through this program.

SMS opt-in and consent

SMS consent is collected from the participant before notifications are enabled. In BabyBat, the participant provides their mobile number, actively enables SMS notifications, and accepts the SMS disclosure shown with the control.

Consent language:
By enabling SMS notifications, I agree to receive recurring transactional text messages from BabyBat Game Hub at the mobile number I provide, including game, Mail, Directive, scoring, reward, status, and account notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is optional and is not required to use BabyBat.

Consent to receive SMS is kept separate from acceptance of BabyBat's general Terms or Privacy Policy.

Sample messages

BabyBat: You have new game Mail waiting. Open BabyBat to review it. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
BabyBat: A new Directive is available. Open BabyBat to review and respond. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
BabyBat: A scoring update was posted to your game. Open BabyBat for details. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Opt-out and help

🛑

STOP: Reply STOP at any time to stop BabyBat SMS notifications. Participants may also disable SMS from their BabyBat notification settings.

ℹ️

HELP: Reply HELP for assistance and information about the BabyBat notification program.

Message frequency varies based on game activity. Carrier message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information & privacy

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent records, and opt-in information are used only to provide requested BabyBat notifications and operate the messaging program. BabyBat does not sell mobile information and does not share mobile information, SMS consent, or opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be provided to service providers strictly as necessary to deliver or operate the messaging service, such as the SMS delivery provider.
