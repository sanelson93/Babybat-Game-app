Nocturnal Games Privacy Policy

Effective: September 16, 2026

Nocturnal Games is a private, invitation-only game platform. This policy describes the information used to operate the app.

Information used

Nocturnal Games may process account identifiers, profile information, game records, messages, submissions, notification preferences, and technical information necessary to provide the service.

Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, Nocturnal Games stores a browser/device push subscription endpoint and cryptographic delivery keys so the app can send game/account alerts to that device. Push can be disabled from your profile or device settings.

Purpose

Information is used to authenticate participants, operate the game, provide requested features, send optional transactional notifications, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service.

Security

Nocturnal Games uses authenticated access controls and private storage for non-public game content. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact & support

Support is provided to participating users through the authenticated Nocturnal Games application.
