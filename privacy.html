BabyBat Privacy Policy

Effective: September 15, 2026

BabyBat Game Hub is a private, invitation-only game platform. This policy describes the information used to operate BabyBat, including optional SMS notifications.

Information used

BabyBat may process account identifiers, profile information, game records, messages, submissions, notification preferences, and mobile phone information supplied by participants.

SMS information

If a participant opts into SMS notifications, BabyBat may store the mobile number, notification preference, and consent/opt-in records necessary to operate the messaging program.

Mobile information, SMS consent, and opt-in data are not sold and are not shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be disclosed to service providers only as necessary to operate BabyBat and deliver requested messages, including hosting/database and SMS delivery providers.

Purpose

Information is used to authenticate participants, operate the game, provide requested app features, send optional transactional notifications, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service.

Choices

SMS is optional. Participants can reply STOP or disable SMS notifications in BabyBat. Participants may reply HELP for assistance.

Security

BabyBat uses authenticated access controls and private storage for non-public game content. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact & support

Support is provided to participating users through the authenticated BabyBat application.
